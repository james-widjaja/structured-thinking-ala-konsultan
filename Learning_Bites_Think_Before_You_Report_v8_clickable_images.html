The question for today

How Could I Become
A Structured Thinker?

What do great problem solvers do differently?

Pak Win note: Pause here. Let participants sit with the question before introducing the first case.
Habit 1

The Chairman's Question

7 PM. An urgent request arrives. A number is needed tomorrow morning. People begin collecting data.

What is really being asked?

Clarify the Context

Before asking “what data do they need?”, ask “what decision is this supporting?”

  • What is the objective?
  • Who is making the decision?
  • What risks are being evaluated?
  • What recommendation would help?
Pak Win note: Case first. Ask the room: “What should we clarify first?” Let people answer. Then introduce clarifying context and decision before collecting data.

Habit 3

Production Is Down 8%

A message is about to be escalated.

How would you make this decision-ready?

Let participants draft or say one sentence first.

Recommend the Action

Management often already knows the issue. What management needs is judgment.

SituationWhat happened?
InsightWhat does it mean?
RecommendationWhat should we do?
RiskWhat could go wrong?
Next StepWho does what by when?
Pak Win note: Ask the room: “What would you send to management?” Invite one weak escalation first, then show how the template makes it decision-ready.
Participant test case

ESG Non-Compliance Fine

Operating Unit C receives a fine for water treatment regulation non-compliance.

What would you recommend to the MD?

Use the three habits

  • Clarify: What decision must leadership make?
  • Structure: How should we break the problem down?
  • Recommend: What action should we bring?

Prompt if needed: Who is impacted? Could this happen elsewhere? Why did controls fail? What should change system-wide?

Pak Win note: This is the practice test. Do not reveal too quickly. Let participants work through the thinking.
Final takeaway

Clarify → Structure → Recommend

1. Before I start

What is the context? What decision is being supported?

2. Before I analyze

How can I break this problem into clear parts?

3. Before I report

What recommendation, risk, and next step will I include?

Starting tomorrow...

The next time I receive a problem, I will first

The next time I send a report, I will include
